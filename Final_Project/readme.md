## Final Machine Learning Project

This paper investigates the influence of gender on voice-based age classification, based on the biological and
acoustic differences of male and female voices in age. We examine whether these gender-specific differences
have an effect on the classification accuracy of machine learning models for age classification. Key concepts
include audio feature extraction and gender-aware modeling approaches. Using the Mozilla Common V oice
dataset with 60,000 recordings across six languages and nine age brackets, 88 numerical audio features were
extracted. Machine learning models such as Random Forest and Multi-Layer Perceptron classifiers were
trained and evaluated, with gender segmented and feature enriched models providing advanced solutions.
Results show that gender-aware models outperform standard classifiers, with female voice models achieving
higher accuracy than male models. The paper concludes that incorporating gender inference enhances age
recognition of voices and recommends further research on more granular age labels.
